# Vision

**Summary.** Genemon turns a player's real life into a living creature. A genome sets the starting
point on 26 opposite-pole axes; time, places, food, friends and battles move the creature through
that space; the result fights in physical 3D arenas and shapes a shared world that keeps existing
while its players are away. It is a personal lifestyle tracker that happens to be a monster game —
or a monster game you can play from the sofa without ever leaving home.

## The player promise

1. **Your Genemon is yours alone.** Two players raising the same genome get two different creatures,
   because the creature is the genome *times* the life it lived (`express(genome, conditions)`).
2. **The real world is at the top of everything.** Local time, season, moon, weather, the device in
   your hand and the places you go feed every system below them: arena light, day/night rhythm buffs,
   spawns, quests, evolution.
3. **Play anywhere, at any time.** Mobile on the move (physical-only actions: gym, supermarket,
   territory, rare spawns), tablet or laptop on the sofa (missions, the virtual space with arenas and
   dungeons), desktop for the deep systems (breeding, data, ladder).
4. **Everything is explainable.** Every number the game uses can be traced: the Lab shows genetic vs
   expressed axes and where life pressure came from; every battle hit carries its full damage
   breakdown in the ledger.

## Pillars

| Pillar | Means in practice |
|---|---|
| Generative, not catalogued | No hand-made species list. Kind, pattern, elements, moves and personality emerge from the genome. |
| Physical | Skeletons with coupled joint limits, mass, density, elasticity, friction, slopes, water, wind. Big and heavy matters. |
| Spatial combat | Distance, area, projectile travel, mid-air clashes, ground transformation, zones that persist. |
| Conditions over levels | Arena, weather, hour and team synergy decide as much as level; the ladder rates players accordingly. |
| AI-native | One event schema (the Ledger) for everything, deterministic seeds, exportable datasets, profiles for humans *and* AIs. |
| Honest | Statuses in `docs/SPEC.md`, measured evidence in `docs/EVIDENCE.md`, nothing claimed that was not observed. |

## Non-goals (for now)

- Real-money trading or minted NFTs. The genome id is a stable content hash (the natural token id),
  but minting and money are legal/regulatory work, not code (see `docs/ECONOMY.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md`).
- Any third-party runtime dependency other than three.js.
